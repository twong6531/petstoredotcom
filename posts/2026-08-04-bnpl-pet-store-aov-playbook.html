BNPL Lifts Pet Store AOV 50% and Conversions 30%. Here's the 2026 Buy Now, Pay Later Playbook.

📅 August 4, 2026 ✍️ PetStore.com Editorial Team 🏷️ Tech · Payments · Ecommerce

A single bag of Hill's Prescription Diet k/d for kidney support runs $95. A premium orthopedic dog bed costs $180 to $350. A Halo collar health monitor is $749. PetSmart already offers Afterpay. Chewy runs Affirm financing on high-ticket items. Synchrony just integrated CareCredit with pet insurance platforms in March 2026. And the average independent pet store still only takes a credit card. That's not a neutral choice — it's costing you sales at the exact price points where your margins are highest.

What BNPL Actually Does to Your Numbers

Buy now, pay later (BNPL) splits a customer's total into 4 interest-free installments — typically every two weeks. For a $180 orthopedic bed, that's four payments of $45. The psychology is not subtle, and the data behind it is hard to argue with.

Merchants who add BNPL see conversion rates increase 20–30% at checkout, according to 2026 aggregated data from Klarna, Afterpay, and Shop Pay Installments. Average order value (AOV) lifts are even more dramatic: 20–50% higher AOVs when BNPL is available, with Shop Pay Installments specifically reporting up to 50% AOV lift over Shopify merchants without it — 18% higher than Afterpay and 26% higher than Sezzle on the same platform.

The sweet spot is purchases in the $100–$500 range, which happens to be where pet retail's highest-margin categories live: premium prescription diets, large-bag raw food cases, smart feeders, health monitors, and high-end bedding. BNPL turns a "$200 feels like a lot to spend right now" hesitation into a "$50 every two weeks for eight weeks" easy yes.

The Pet Store BNPL Opportunity Is Bigger Than You Think

There's a whole vertical dedicated to pet BNPL that most store owners haven't heard of: veterinary financing. The Veterinary Financing BNPL Solutions Market was valued at $1.24 billion in 2026 and is projected to reach $2.42 billion by 2030 at an 18.3% CAGR. That growth is driven by rising emergency vet bills (averaging $1,500–$5,000 for major procedures), increasing pet ownership — U.S. pet-owning households grew from 82 million in 2023 to 94 million in early 2025 — and a generation of owners who expect payment flexibility as a baseline, not a perk.

That willingness to use installment payments flows directly into retail. Pet shoppers already have the mental model. They've used CareCredit at the vet. They've split an Afterpay order at PetSmart. When they land on your product page for a $160 case of Purina Pro Plan Veterinary Diets and there's no installment option, you're not just losing a sale — you're handing them a reason to click over to a store that does offer it.

One more number worth noting: pet care has the lowest cart abandonment rate of any tracked retail category at 54.78%, compared to 70%+ for most ecommerce. Your customers have high purchase intent by nature. BNPL doesn't manufacture desire — it removes the last friction point between a motivated buyer and a completed order.

The Four BNPL Providers Worth Considering

You don't need all of them. Pick one and implement it well. Here's the breakdown of the main options in 2026:

Shop Pay Installments (powered by Affirm) — This is the default recommendation for any Shopify store. It's built into Shopify Payments — activation takes under 10 minutes in your admin panel, no separate app install. Merchant fee is 5.9% + $0.30. The AOV lift data is the strongest of any provider (up to 50%). Shop Pay's broader installments program is approaching $8 billion in annualized gross merchandise volume. If you're on Shopify and not using this, you're genuinely leaving money on the table.

Klarna — The largest BNPL provider by merchant distribution, holding 74.6% of market share in early 2026. Merchant fee starts at a lower 3.29% + $0.30 — the best fee structure of the major players. Brand recognition is high, especially with Millennial and Gen Z shoppers. Available via the Shopify App Store with an easy install. A solid alternative if you want to negotiate lower fees or if your customer base skews heavily toward shoppers who already use Klarna.

Afterpay — The choice for stores focused on younger shoppers. Afterpay has a loyal Gen Z user base, and it's already proven in the pet vertical through PetSmart's partnership. Merchant fee runs 4–6% + $0.30. Important caveat: Afterpay does not refund the merchant fee on returns, so factor that into your return policy math before activating it on high-return categories.

Sezzle — Higher fees (6% + $0.30) than the others, but strong integration support and a useful social impact angle — Sezzle lets shoppers round up payments to donate to charity, which resonates with the values-driven pet owner demographic. Smaller market share (4.4%) means less brand recognition, but it can be a good fit for stores with a strong community identity.

What to Put BNPL On — and What to Leave Off

BNPL is not a "turn it on everywhere and watch the money flow" feature. The merchant fees (3.3–6%) are 1.5–4x higher than standard card processing (2.9% + $0.30). On a $12 bag of training treats with a 25% margin, that fee structure destroys profitability. Be strategic about where you surface it.

Strong candidates for BNPL:

  • Prescription and veterinary diet bags ($60–$200+ each)
  • Orthopedic and memory foam pet beds ($120–$400)
  • GPS trackers and health monitors — Halo, Fi Series 4, Whistle Go ($180–$750)
  • Starter kit bundles you've assembled ($80–$250)
  • Raw food case orders and freeze-dried bulk purchases ($90–$180)
  • Smart feeders and connected pet tech ($80–$300)

Most platforms let you set a minimum cart threshold — $50 or $75 — to prevent BNPL from appearing on low-AOV, low-margin purchases. Use it. The conversion lift is real on high-ticket items; on a $14 catnip toy it just eats your profit.

Installing BNPL in Under 30 Minutes on Shopify

For Shop Pay Installments: go to Settings → Payments → Shopify Payments → Manage → toggle on Shop Pay Installments. Done. Shopify automatically adds the "4 payments of $X.XX with Shop Pay" messaging below the price on product pages — no code required.

For Klarna or Afterpay: install their official apps from the Shopify App Store, connect your account, and set your minimum order threshold. Both auto-insert on-site messaging widgets on product pages and in the cart. The messaging widget alone — showing "Split into 4 interest-free payments of $X" under the product price — has been shown to increase conversion even before shoppers reach checkout, because it reframes the price at the moment of consideration.

One critical implementation step most stores skip: add a line to your FAQ and product descriptions calling out that BNPL is available. "Eligible for 4 interest-free payments with Klarna" on a prescription diet product page signals to the customer that you've thought about affordability, which builds trust beyond just the payment option itself.

The Fee Math — It Works If You're Strategic

The concern most owners raise is the fee differential. Let's run the numbers honestly.

On a $180 orthopedic bed at a 40% margin ($72 gross profit), the cost difference between a standard card (2.9% + $0.30 = $5.52) and Shop Pay Installments (5.9% + $0.30 = $10.92) is $5.40. Your net profit drops from $66.48 to $61.08 per unit sold through BNPL.

But that only matters if you assume the same conversion rate. If BNPL converts 20–30% more of the shoppers who reach your product page — and the data says it does — then you're selling more beds per 100 visitors. At 25 sales per 100 visitors without BNPL versus 31 sales with it (a 24% lift), the BNPL channel generates $1,893.48 net vs. $1,662.00 without it. You're $231.48 ahead even after paying higher fees on every transaction.

The math gets better as your AOV goes up. Do it properly on your best-selling high-ticket items and the fees become a cost of growth, not a drag on margin.

What PetSmart and Chewy Are Already Doing

PetSmart's Afterpay partnership is live in-store and online, making installment payments available on everything from kibble to grooming tools to pet furniture. Chewy runs Affirm on higher-ticket purchases, with monthly payment plans for orders over $150. Independent stores have one massive advantage here: you can activate Shop Pay Installments or Klarna today. Not after a 6-month enterprise procurement cycle. Not after an RFP and legal review. Today. A solo operator with a Shopify store can have BNPL live before dinner. The window to get ahead of your regional competitors on this is still open — but it won't be for much longer as awareness spreads after SuperZoo 2026.
