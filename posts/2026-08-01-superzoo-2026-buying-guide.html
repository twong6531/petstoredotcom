SuperZoo 2026 Opens August 12. Here's the Indie Pet Store Buying Guide to the 1,100 New Products Dropping This Month.

📅 August 1, 2026 ✍️ PetStore.com Editorial Team 🏷️ Strategy · Trade Shows · Buying

Eleven days from now, more than 1,300 exhibitors will descend on the Mandalay Bay Convention Center in Las Vegas and debut over 1,100 brand-new pet products in three days. That's the signal, not the noise. SuperZoo is where next quarter's bestsellers get discovered — and most indie pet store owners either don't attend, don't know how to work the show, or miss the post-show buying window entirely. Here's how not to be that store.

What SuperZoo Actually Is (And Why Indie Buyers Have the Advantage)

SuperZoo is the largest U.S. retail pet industry trade show, run by the World Pet Association, and it's trade-only — no general public, no end consumers. That's the point. It's a concentrated buying event where manufacturers are desperate to get into independent retail channels and buyers have real negotiating leverage.

The 2026 edition runs August 12–14 at Mandalay Bay, with education sessions starting August 11. The show floor spans 350,000 square feet, organized into specialty pavilions including Natural and Health, Emerging Brands, and Specialty and Lifestyle. Of the 1,300+ exhibitors, 81% are dog and cat focused — which is your entire addressable market.

Here's the part that matters: large chains like Petco and PetSmart send teams of category managers who have to navigate internal procurement processes, committee approvals, and shelf-space constraints. An indie store owner with a credit card and real authority? You can shake a hand, see a sample, write an opening order, and have product in transit before the big chains have even finished their internal review meetings.

The 7 Award Categories That Tell You Where the Market Is Heading

SuperZoo's New Product Showcase Awards are the show's most important signal. The WPA redesigned the categories for 2026 to reflect what consumers are actually buying. Each category is a green light that the market wants more product in this space:

  • Smart Technology — connected devices, health monitors, GPS, automated feeders
  • Wellness Care — supplements, functional chews, preventive health products
  • Functional Nutrition — ingredient-forward food, targeted formulas, condition-specific diets
  • Sustainability — eco packaging, sustainable proteins, recyclable accessories
  • Safety and Mobility — harnesses, car restraints, ortho products, rehab supports
  • Enrichment — puzzle feeders, lick mats, sensory toys, interactive play
  • Training — reward-based tools, clickers, treat pouches, e-collar alternatives

Notice what's not there: generic kibble, basic collars, commodity toys. The show organizers redesigned these categories because that's where consumer spending is accelerating. If you're building out your 2026–2027 catalog strategy, this list is your framework.

The Emerging Brands Pavilion: Where You Find the Next Big Thing

This is the most underrated section of the show floor, and it's where experienced indie buyers spend a disproportionate amount of their time. The Emerging Brands Pavilion showcases early-stage companies across food, wellness, technology, accessories, and everyday pet care — brands that are too new to have Chewy distribution, too small to be in PetSmart planograms, and actively hunting retail partners who will give them shelf space and genuine merchandising attention.

These brands are also negotiable in ways established players aren't. Opening order minimums are lower. Terms are more flexible. Co-op marketing budgets are available. And the brands that break out of Emerging Brands status into mainstream distribution typically generate enormous press and social media attention in the 12–18 months after the show — which benefits every retailer who got in early.

A confirmed example for 2026: Spoiled Pets, a freeze-dried nutrition brand, has been accepted into both the Emerging Brands Pavilion and the New Products Showcase. Their Spoiled Kitty® Complete Meal — Chicken & Salmon Bowl uses just 12 whole-food, responsibly sourced ingredients. That's exactly the kind of clean-label cat product that is in severe undersupply at independent stores right now. Get their contact information before the show if possible; they'll have buyers lined up three deep at the booth.

Brands and Products Already Confirmed for August 12

Pre-show announcements have already surfaced several products worth getting in front of. Based on what's been confirmed:

Fera Pets, a vet-founded pet wellness brand, is unveiling two new canine innovations in the supplement space: Longevity+, formulated for healthy aging and cellular vitality, and Muscle & Metabolism Support, targeting lean muscle and metabolic health. This is the kind of science-backed supplement positioning that resonates with the health-conscious owner segment — and these products will autoship beautifully.

BetterBone is showcasing the BetterBone GO alongside a full brand refresh. BetterBone has built a strong following in the natural chew category, and new product extensions from brands with existing loyal audiences are reliably easier to sell than cold launches.

Vetericyn is launching a line of Relief Kits — targeted for itchy and irritated skin, eyes, and ears. Vetericyn already has strong veterinary credibility; these kits are the kind of high-margin, needs-driven product that converts immediately when paired with the right in-store or online copy.

JoyFull is entering the functional treat and supplement space with vet-formulated products made from real protein and clean ingredients, with a focus on gut health. Given that 56% of dog owners now actively seek digestive health claims on pet products, this positions squarely on the fastest-growing benefit platform in pet nutrition.

Atomic Bubbles is lighter fare — non-toxic Puppy Love and Kitty Love Bubbles, pairing gingerbread-scented bubbles with training treats. These aren't going to move serious revenue, but they're the kind of impulse-buy product that lifts average order value and photographs beautifully for social.

How to Work the Show as a Buyer (Even If It's Your First Time)

SuperZoo runs 9am–5pm Tuesday through Thursday, August 12–14. Education sessions (free with registration) run August 11–12. Here's the framework that experienced indie buyers use:

Day One is for discovery. Walk every aisle. Don't stop at booths you already know. The Emerging Brands section runs the full length of the hall — budget at least 90 minutes for it. Collect business cards and product sheets from anything that catches your eye, but don't place orders yet. Your first pass is research.

Day Two is for depth. Go back to your top 15–20 prospects. Ask for samples if they have them, negotiate opening order minimums, and get specific on lead times and stock availability. Ask every brand directly: "Do you have distribution with Chewy yet?" If the answer is no, that's a window.

Day Three is for orders. Finalize your top 8–10 picks. The last day of the show is also when brands are most motivated to close deals — they don't want to pack product home. First-order concessions (extra samples, free shipping, extended payment terms) are common on day three.

One practical note: the 39,000 sq ft New Products Showcase is in its own section of the hall and is worth at least an hour of dedicated time. Products are organized by category and the award finalists are flagged — use that as a shortcut for identifying the industry's best bets.

How to Capitalize on SuperZoo Without Setting Foot in Las Vegas

Not every store owner can or should attend. If you can't make it this year, the show still works for you — you just have to be intentional about it.

First: follow trade publications in real time. Pet Age, Pet Business, PetfoodIndustry.com, and Pet Product News all publish daily show coverage starting August 12. The New Product Showcase Award winners are announced publicly and typically covered within hours. Build a Google Alert for "SuperZoo 2026" starting Monday.

Second: contact the award winners directly by August 15. Every brand that wins or finalists in the New Product Showcase is actively looking for retail distribution — reach out to the three or four that fit your store's positioning and ask about wholesale terms. You'll have the advantage of striking while the show momentum is still fresh.

Third: use the Emerging Brands list as a prospecting tool. SuperZoo publishes its accepted Emerging Brand exhibitors on their website. Go through the list this week, identify the food, supplement, and wellness brands, and reach out before the show. Brands that haven't yet landed their first major retail relationships are especially motivated before a trade show — you can often get better terms pre-show than post.

The Products You Should Be Building Autoship Around Right Now

Here's the strategic framing that connects SuperZoo to your revenue engine: the products that dominate trade shows — functional nutrition, wellness supplements, condition-specific formulas — are also the products that autoship at the highest rates.

A customer who subscribes to a monthly joint supplement for their senior Lab or a weekly probiotic topper for their sensitive-stomach cat is not comparison shopping every order. They found something that works, and they set it and forget it. That's an LTV of $300–800 per year from a single SKU relationship.

When you're walking the SuperZoo floor next week — or reviewing the award winners from your desk — ask this question about every product: would a pet owner reorder this every 30–60 days? If yes, it belongs in your catalog and it belongs in your autoship program. Consumables with functional benefits are the backbone of subscription pet retail, and SuperZoo is dropping 1,100 new candidates for that role this month.

The Window Is Open — For About Three Weeks

The pattern at every SuperZoo is the same: brands announce their launches at the show, get coverage in trade press, and within 30–45 days, the best-positioned ones have their first batch of retail orders confirmed. By the time holiday buying ramps up in October, the early-mover slots are filled.

If you move on one or two well-chosen new brands this month, you'll have them on your shelves — and in your autoship catalog — before your competitors have even heard of them. That's the whole game.
