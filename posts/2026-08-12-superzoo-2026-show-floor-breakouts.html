SuperZoo 2026 Is Happening Right Now. Here Are the 8 Breakout Products Every Indie Store Should Order This Week.

📅 August 12, 2026 ✍️ PetStore.com Editorial Team 🏷️ Strategy · SuperZoo · New Products

By 3 p.m. Pacific today, the doors at Mandalay Bay open and more than 20,000 pet retail buyers start walking a show floor with 1,300+ exhibitors and over 1,100 products that haven't hit a single store shelf yet. If you're not in Las Vegas — and most indie store owners aren't — here's what you need to know before your competitors place their orders.

SuperZoo is North America's largest pet industry trade show. What gets launched here in August lands in stores in October. What sells at those booths this week determines what's on your shelf — or your competitor's shelf — for holiday gifting, Q4 peak season, and Q1 reorders. Waiting until the industry recap lands in your inbox next week means waiting until everyone else already ordered.

We tracked the pre-show announcements, New Product Showcase entries, and brand reveals so you don't have to. Here are the eight launches worth your attention right now — and what they mean for your buying strategy.

Why 2026 Is the Most Competitive SuperZoo in History

The context matters. Pet care is expected to reach $95.3 billion in 2026, and the industry is no longer just growing — it's bifurcating. Commodity pet food is getting squeezed by price sensitivity and private label. Meanwhile, premium, functional, and wellness-positioned products are outperforming every benchmark.

That bifurcation shows up on the SuperZoo floor. Natural and health products now account for 33.2% of all exhibitors — up 5.8% from 2025. The New Product Showcase categories were updated this year to explicitly include smart technology, wellness care, functional nutrition, sustainability, safety and mobility, enrichment, and training. That's not a coincidence. It's a map of where the industry is going.

IndiePet — the association representing independent pet retailers — hosts a sponsored breakfast on the show floor specifically to give indie stores access to the same intelligence that the big chains walk in with. If you're not a member, this week is a good reminder to sign up.

Protein-Forward Treats: Small Dogs Finally Get Their Moment

Three Dog Bakery® is arriving at SuperZoo with seven new product innovations spanning three bets: protein-forward treats, people-inspired formats, and a category that's been chronically underserved — small dog-focused products.

That third angle is the real business insight. Small dogs represent nearly half of U.S. dog ownership, but the treat aisle has historically been built for Labs and Goldens. Appropriately sized, correctly portioned treats for toy and small breeds are a white space most stores haven't filled intentionally. Three Dog Bakery's small dog line changes that calculus.

For your order: stock at least two small-dog-forward SKUs from this launch alongside your standard sizes. The customer who has a Chihuahua or a Shih Tzu and finally finds treats sized for their dog becomes a loyal, repeat buyer. The one who feels ignored by the treat aisle keeps ordering from Amazon.

Longevity Is the New Puppy Stage

Fera Pets, a vet-founded wellness brand, is unveiling two specific innovations at SuperZoo that tell you everything about where the supplement market is heading: Longevity+ (targeting healthy aging and cellular vitality) and Muscle & Metabolism Support (for lean muscle and metabolic health).

Both products speak to a macro shift the industry has been watching for years and is now fully arriving: the senior pet explosion. Over half of U.S. dogs and cats are now classified as seniors, and their owners are increasingly aware of, and willing to pay for, products that extend quality of life rather than just treat disease.

The pet dietary supplement market hit $5.11 billion in 2026 and is tracking to $7.31 billion by 2031 at 7.42% CAGR. The fastest-growing functional areas within that market are longevity, mobility, and metabolic support. Fera's SuperZoo launches are a direct product of that demand signal.

Practically: if you sell supplements and you don't have a "healthy aging" section — even three to five SKUs explicitly positioned for senior dogs and cats — you're leaving the highest-LTV customer in pet retail without a reason to come back monthly. Longevity supplements are autoship gold. Owners who start a senior dog on a cellular support formula don't stop ordering it.

Enrichment Has Left the Building As a Trend — It's Now a Category

Brightkins is debuting the Kanoodle Pyramid Puzzle at SuperZoo: four spinning tiers, 22 hidden treat compartments, and enough complexity to keep most dogs occupied for meaningful stretches of time. They're also previewing the Brightkins Talking Buttons — launching January 2027, but generating retailer interest now at the show.

Separately, BetterBone is showcasing the BetterBone GO alongside new lower price points designed to remove the last barrier for skeptical first-time buyers.

Both brands are showing up in a category that has moved from "interesting add-on" to "must-stock." Industry data confirms it: puzzle toys, functional treats, and long-lasting chews are now consistently strong year-round sellers — not seasonal spikes. The word that keeps appearing in buying reports is "necessity." Pet parents have internalized the veterinary advice about mental stimulation and are actively looking for new challenges to rotate through. That's a repeat-purchase engine.

Action item: if your enrichment section is still one shelf with three products, double it before October. Then set up a "New This Month" rotation in that section so customers have a reason to check it on every visit.

Gut Health Gets a New Delivery Format

JoyFull is on the SuperZoo floor with vet-formulated functional treats and supplements that lead on real protein and clean ingredients — with gut health as the primary claim. The twist: JoyFull's format blurs the line between treat and supplement, making it easier for pet owners to actually give their animals the gut support they're looking for without adding a separate pill or powder to the routine.

Why this matters: 56% of dog owners and 54% of cat owners are actively seeking "digestive health" claims on pet food products. That's the highest consumer demand signal in the supplement category. But compliance — whether the owner actually gives the supplement consistently — has always been the gap. A treat format that delivers the functional benefit closes that gap.

Stock JoyFull near your probiotic and digestive supplement shelf. Merchandised together, the "choose your format" approach gives the customer a path to gut health they'll actually stick with — which means repeat orders every four to six weeks.

A 100-Year-Old Brand Just Showed You the Hole in Your Kibble Aisle

Midwestern Pet Foods is celebrating its centennial at SuperZoo by launching new products that reveal a genuine gap in most indie store kibble sections: right-sized bags for specific diet customers.

Their Wholesomes Sensitive Skin & Stomach, Beef Meal & Rice, and Whitefish Meal & Rice formulas are now available in 12-pound bags — a size that works for smaller dogs, rotation feeders, and owners who want to trial a sensitive formula before committing to a 30-pound bag. They're also introducing Wholesomes Rewards Chewy Treats: soft training treats available in multiple proteins, shapes, and sizes.

The insight here isn't really about Wholesomes specifically. It's that customers with dogs on limited-ingredient or sensitive diets are often your most loyal, highest-frequency buyers — and they struggle to find their specific formula in an appropriate bag size. If your sensitive skin/stomach section only offers giant bags, you're filtering out a significant segment of buyers who need that formula but don't want to commit to a 30-pound bag they haven't tried. Add the 12-pound tier. Watch your trial rate on premium diet foods improve immediately.

Natural Now Owns One-Third of the Show Floor — What That Means for Your Margins

The most important number from SuperZoo 2026 isn't a product launch. It's the exhibitor composition: natural and health products now account for 33.2% of all 1,300+ exhibitors — up 5.8% year-over-year. That's not a trend. That's a structural shift in what the industry is making and what consumers are demanding.

For independent pet stores, this creates both an opportunity and a warning. The opportunity: natural and premium products carry better margins, attract customers with higher LTV, and create differentiation that Chewy and Amazon can't replicate with their commodity-optimized catalogs. The warning: as more brands make the move to "natural" positioning, the floor is getting crowded and ingredient claims are getting murkier.

Your job as a buyer is to demand specificity from your vendors. "Natural" alone is not a differentiator anymore. "Single-source protein, grass-fed, U.S.-sourced, with third-party testing" — that's a differentiator. Tighten your buying criteria for any new natural SKU you add post-SuperZoo. Your curation is the thing customers can't get from an algorithm.

How to Order the Right Products Without Being on the Show Floor

SuperZoo runs through August 14. Most brands showcase new products at the show and immediately open order books for Q4 inventory. Here's how to move quickly even from your desk:

  • Contact your distributors today. Most major pet distributors — Pet Food Experts, Phillips Pet, Central Garden & Pet — have buyers walking the SuperZoo floor. Email your rep now with the brands on this list and ask for availability windows and minimum order quantities.
  • Go direct for brands you already carry. If you have a direct account with any of these brands, their sales teams are at the show and checking email. A quick "I want to pre-order the SuperZoo launches" email gets attention this week when their pipelines are warm.
  • Follow the New Product Showcase results. SuperZoo announces New Product Showcase winners during the show. Winners get outsized trade press coverage, which drives consumer demand within 60–90 days. Whatever wins this year deserves to be on your shelf by October.
  • Check IndiePet resources. IndiePet compiles show takeaways and buying intelligence for independent retailers specifically. Their post-show reports are worth the membership cost alone during SuperZoo week.

The fastest-moving SuperZoo launches typically sell through distributor pre-orders before the show floor closes. This is not a situation where waiting for the recap serves you well.

The Bottom Line

SuperZoo 2026 is confirming what the smartest pet retailers already know: the game is won in the premium, functional, and wellness tiers — and the brands showing up with longevity supplements, appropriately sized treats, enrichment tools, and gut-health formats are the ones whose products your best customers will be asking for by name in October.

You don't need to be on the show floor to act on this intelligence. You need to be on the phone with your distributor before the week is out.
